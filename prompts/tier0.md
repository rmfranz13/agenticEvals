You are a guidance, navigation and control engineer. Write flight software that lands a 2-D lunar lander, starting from lunar orbit, at a designated landing site.

The full specification is in /workspace/docs/spec.md. Read it first.

Write your flight software in /workspace/controller.py. You can fly it against the simulator in /workspace/sim with:

    python sim/run_sim.py controller.py --seeds 0 10

Your controller will be graded on hidden scenarios drawn from the same dispersion distributions as the dev seeds, so a controller that only works on the seeds you tested is not enough.

When you are confident in your controller, call submit() with a short summary of your guidance and control approach. Only the contents of /workspace/controller.py at that point are graded.
